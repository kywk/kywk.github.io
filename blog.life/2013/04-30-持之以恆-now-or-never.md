# 持之以恆！ Now or never !

<!--truncate-->

看到小兔的[說好要持之以恆！](http://goo.gl/rrQwx)，想起去年訂定的[目標](http://goo.gl/j27Bs)，當然沒做到的日子居多。  

想起明年要迎接的變化，重新擬定目標。有所作為，無論公事、私事。**持之以恆！**  

![](http://1.bp.blogspot.com/-dFcfND7BJ4g/UKst9UPq4yI/AAAAAAAAArc/42idsZnzszo/s640/373827_2106441879056_1458775094_n.jpg)

  

  

- Daily

  - A new blog article at kywk.life, foto-studio or endroid. (Include finish a marked draft or followup article)

  - One imaging work: photoshop, web layout design, css component, etc... 

- Weekly

  - Regular planning

    - Monday: PTT BM running, 10k / trip: India

    - Tuesday:  Interval training 60min / trip: SriLanka

    - Wednesday: [Taipei Metro Hash House Harriers](http://goo.gl/q2OGH) or [Puma night-run training](http://goo.gl/GQN91) / trip: Nelap (Annapurna)

    - Thursday: 1.5hr Body Combat / trip: Japan

    - Friday: trip: Euro

  - Read one book and write down review at least.

  - Finish a foto set and its article.

  - A algorithm implementation or a ACM programs.

  - A technique post in endroid.

  - Translate a article. (photograph, information technique, etc...)

- Monthly

  - (Every two weeks)Cook a meal recipes. Include salad, starter, main-dish, dessert.

  - Publish a simple app (webapp, android, iphone, scripts, util, lib, etc...) 

  - Mount climb, little trip in Taiwan.

- Quarterly

  - Little trip to East Taiwan or outside main island.

  - A simple open source project.

  - Least one 42km marathon.

- Yearly

  - A foreign 42km marathon.

  - One mid-time wander to foreign.
